# Bouquin: product one-pager

**What it is:** Tracks the books r/suggestmeabook keeps recommending: every title named in the comments, matched to Open Library for a cover and a genre, counted each time another comment names it, and linked back to the threads.

**Who it is for:** A reader who wants their next book and trusts a crowd of strangers more than an algorithm, but has no time to read three hundred comments a day to find out which four titles everyone keeps naming.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Evening, phone or laptop, the current book just finished. Two minutes of scrolling covers, a tap on one to see who suggested it and in reply to what, then off to the library app. Dark theme, cover-first, dense enough that a screen shows a dozen candidates.

**What done looks like:** The visitor leaves with one title they had not heard of and the thread that made the case for it.
